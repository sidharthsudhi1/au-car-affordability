Assessing the affordability of cars in Australia

How many new cars a year's median pay buys, by segment, state and worker group. Earnings have outpaced quality-adjusted car prices since 2014, but part-time workers and smaller states remain far behind.

Segment-wise affordability timeline

Affordability Index (AI) = annual median earnings / median new price in each segment. An AI of 1.5 means a year's pay buys one and a half cars. The marker follows the year slider.

Segment prices are 2023 median new listings moved through time with the ABS motor vehicles index. Source: ABS Employee Earnings (Aug), ABS CPI; Kaggle Australian Vehicle Prices.

Design process

This page realises sheet 5 of a Five Design Sheet process: brainstorm, three alternative designs, then the final layout with its operations (year slider, segment filter, state click, flow hover).

Built with D3 from tables produced by a tested Python and R pipeline. Car prices: ABS motor vehicles CPI (quality adjusted) anchored to 2023 listings. Earnings: ABS Employee Earnings. Listings are asking prices from a single 2023 snapshot. State boundaries: Natural Earth.
